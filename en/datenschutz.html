03Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you open a page, your browser transmits technically necessary data to GitHub's servers, where it is processed in server log files. This includes in particular:

  • your IP address
  • the date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested
  • the volume of data transferred and the HTTP status code
  • browser type, browser version and operating system
  • where applicable, the previously visited page (referrer)

This processing is technically necessary in order to deliver the website and to keep its operation secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in providing the website reliably and securely.

We ourselves have no access to these log files and do not analyse them. For further information, please see GitHub's privacy statement: GitHub Privacy Statement.

04Fonts and External Resources

This page embeds no external fonts, scripts or tracking services. Only the system fonts already present on your device are used.

Opening this page therefore establishes no connections to third-party servers, apart from the hosting server that delivers the page itself.

05Contact Form

A contact form is available on the home page. If you use it, we process the data you enter:

  • subject of your enquiry (selection)
  • name
  • email address
  • message text

We use this data solely to handle and respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry relates to entering into or performing a contract, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Technical delivery is handled by the service Web3Forms (Web3Creative). The provider processes the submitted form data on our behalf as a processor and forwards it to us by email. The provider operates its infrastructure using, among others, Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner; processing may therefore also take place outside the European Union. Further information is available at web3forms.com/privacy.

Providing your data is voluntary. You may write to us directly by email instead at any time.

06Contacting Us by Email

If you write to us by email, we process your email address and the information contained in your message in order to respond to your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.

Our mailbox is operated via Google Mail (Google Ireland Limited). Processing by that provider may therefore occur.

07No Cookies, No Tracking

This website sets no cookies. There is no web analytics, no profiling, no audience measurement and no advertising.

For this reason, the site also requires no consent banner.

08International Data Transfers

Where data is transferred to service providers established or operating infrastructure outside the European Economic Area – specifically GitHub (USA) for hosting and Web3Forms when the contact form is used – such transfers take place on the basis of the applicable safeguards under Chapter V GDPR, in particular standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision where one exists.

An absolute equivalence with the level of data protection within the EU cannot be guaranteed in every case.

09Retention Period

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes described above or for as long as statutory retention obligations apply.

Enquiries received via the contact form or by email are deleted once the matter has been dealt with conclusively and no statutory retention periods prevent deletion.

10Rights of Data Subjects

Subject to the statutory requirements, you have the following rights:

  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)

An informal message to onpoy.studios@gmail.com is sufficient to exercise these rights.
