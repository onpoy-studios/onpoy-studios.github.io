02Subject Matter of the App

OnPoy Cars is a toy-style car racing game. You race one of many cars against computer-controlled opponents on various tracks (levels), collect stars and use them to unlock more levels.

The App contains in particular:

  • A selection of different cars
  • Race tracks (levels) with loops, ramps, jumps and obstacles
  • A workshop where you customize the look of your cars (paint, tires, stickers, exhaust)
  • Settings for music, sounds, haptics, graphics, opponent difficulty and language

All opponents are controlled by the game on your device. There is no online multiplayer mode and no way to communicate with other people in the App.

Your stars, unlocked levels and workshop customizations are stored only locally on your device. There is no entitlement to transfer game progress to another device or to restore it after deleting the App.

How many stars you receive in a level depends on your finishing position in the race. The conditions for unlocking further levels may change as the App is developed further.

The cars, tracks and places in the App are fictional, toy-style game content and do not represent real vehicles, race tracks or places.
