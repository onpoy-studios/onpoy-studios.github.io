02General Information About the App and Data Processing

OnPoy Cars is a toy-style car racing game for iPhone and iPad. You race against computer-controlled opponents, collect stars, unlock levels and customize your cars in the workshop. No registration and no user account are required. We do not operate a backend or server of our own for game progress.

The app itself does not collect, in particular:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Photos or videos
  • Voice recordings

The app does not use the camera or microphone and does not ask for your precise location.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no third-party software that collects data. No data is transmitted to us or to third parties while you play.

03Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your device (via the iOS feature UserDefaults):

  • Collected stars and the best star rating per level
  • Unlocked levels
  • Last selected car and level
  • Workshop customizations per car (paint, tires, stickers, exhaust)
  • Settings: music, music volume, sounds, haptics, graphics, opponent difficulty and language
  • Whether the introduction has already been shown on first launch

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us and not shared with third parties. We do not keep a copy on any server.

You can remove all of this data by deleting the app.

04Language and Region Settings

The app reads the language set on your device to choose a suitable language automatically: German if your device language is German, otherwise English. No access to GPS or your precise location is required.

You can change the language in the app's settings at any time. Your choice is stored locally on the device (see Section 3).

05No Advertising and No Tracking

OnPoy Cars is ad-free. In particular, the app has:

  • No advertisements
  • No third-party advertising or analytics services (for example, no Google AdMob)
  • No use of the advertising identifier (IDFA) and no App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No cookies

The app does not need an internet connection to play. The intro video, the music and all game content are included in the app.

06No In-App Purchases

OnPoy Cars contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. All cars, tracks and workshop customizations can be played for free.

The app is completely free – with no advertising and no purchases. The app does not process any payment data.

07No User Accounts and No Online Features

You do not need a user account to use the app. In particular, there is:

  • No login
  • No public profiles
  • No chat
  • No messages between players
  • No way to upload photos or videos
  • No online multiplayer mode

The opponents in a race are controlled entirely by the game on your device. There is no connection to other players.

08Disclosure of Personal Data

The app does not transmit any personal data to us or to third parties. We do not sell data and do not disclose data unless we are legally required to do so.

Apple processes its own data when you download the app from the App Store and when you use iOS. Apple is responsible for this itself (see Section 17).

If you have allowed iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with aggregated crash reports and usage statistics. These do not allow any conclusions about you as a person. You can turn this off at any time under "Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements".

09International Data Transfers

We ourselves do not transfer any personal data to countries outside the European Economic Area.

Apple's privacy policy applies to data processing by Apple in connection with the App Store and iOS (see Section 17).

10Legal Bases for Processing

The game and settings data (Section 3) is stored only on your device because it is strictly necessary for the game to work. This is permitted without consent under Section 25(2) No. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG). This data does not leave your device.

If you contact us by email, we process your message and your email address to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in answering inquiries. Where we are legally obliged to process data, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR applies.

11Retention Period and Deletion

Your stars, unlocked levels, workshop customizations and settings remain on your device until you delete the app. Since we do not store them on our own servers, we do not have a copy.

We delete email inquiries once they have been fully handled, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

12Children

OnPoy Cars is a game for children and is listed in the App Store's Kids category for ages 6–8. That is why we designed the app to be especially data-minimal:

  • No advertising and no advertising or analytics services
  • No collection of personal data by the app
  • No in-app purchases
  • No user accounts, no profiles, no chat
  • No links to websites or other apps

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If a child writes to us by email, we use the information only to reply and delete it afterwards. Parents can contact us at any time (see Section 19).

More information for parents can be found in the "Guide for Parents" in the app.

13No Use of Camera, Microphone or Location

OnPoy Cars does not need access to the following for the game:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos
  • Contacts
  • Location

The app therefore does not ask for any of these permissions.

14Your Rights

Where the GDPR applies, you have the following rights in particular:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR)

Since game progress and settings are stored only locally on your device and we do not maintain user accounts, we cannot attribute this data to a person or delete it remotely. You can remove it yourself at any time by deleting the app.

15Right to Lodge a Complaint with a Supervisory Authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority if you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes the GDPR (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen). You may also contact the supervisory authority where you live.

This right exists without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedy.

16Security

We rely on data minimization: there are deliberately no user accounts, no advertising, no third-party services and no storage of game progress on servers. Data that is never collected cannot fall into the wrong hands.
