01Dear parents and guardians

This guide is meant to help you get to know “Onpoys MiniGames” and make sure your child uses it safely and in an age-appropriate way.

02What is Onpoys MiniGames?

“Onpoys MiniGames” is a colourful party game with 25 short 3D mini-games for iPhone and iPad. A round usually lasts less than a minute. The games include, for example, Bomb Party, Cake Tower, Snowball Fight and Ice Floe Hop.

All games have a cheerful cartoon style. There is no blood and no realistic violence. Characters tumble off platforms or fall into the water in a funny way. Some mini-games include playful elements such as water pistols, snowballs, toy bombs or friendly ghosts.

03What age is the App suitable for?

We designed the App for children and teenagers aged 13 and over. Besides the game content, we took particular account of the fact that online mode allows playing against people your child does not know.

The age rating shown in the App Store is determined according to Apple's guidelines and may be displayed differently depending on the region. There is no separate rating by the German USK.

04Offline and online mode

  • Offline, your child plays against two computer opponents. No internet connection is needed, and no game data is sent to other players.
  • Online, your child plays via Apple Game Center against random players or invited friends. Other players can see, in particular, the Game Center name, the custom avatar and the gameplay. Technically, game-related data such as player ID, movements and scores is also exchanged between the devices.

The App itself has no chat, no voice transmission and no way to send photos, videos or messages to other players. Apple Game Center features outside the App (for example friend requests) are provided by Apple and can be restricted via Screen Time.

05Hearts in online mode

Each online match uses up one heart (maximum 4). Hearts refill by themselves, currently one every 30 minutes. Hearts cannot be bought.

The heart system is a game rule, not a parental control. If you want to limit your child's playing time, please use Screen Time (see below).

06Advertising

The App is free and financed by advertising. Occasionally a full-screen ad appears after offline games; there are also optional rewarded videos that give an extra heart.

By default, we treat all users as minors: no personalised advertising, no tracking prompt, and only ads suitable for the App's age rating (13+). For this we use Google's “PG” rating (comparable to “parental guidance recommended”).

Only from 16 and only with proof: On iOS 26 and later, the App asks Apple for your child's age range. You can decide in Family Sharing whether it is shared. Only if Apple reports that someone is at least 16, and this age was set by the parents or confirmed, do a consent dialog and the iOS tracking prompt appear. Nothing changes for children under 16. (In countries without mandatory age verification, for example outside Europe, the age request does not apply; the usual consent dialog applies there.) However, we cannot guarantee that every single ad is suitable for every child.

If your child taps an ad, a website or the App Store may open. Talk to your child about the fact that advertising is designed to make people buy things.

07No purchases

The App currently contains no in-app purchases. We still recommend restricting App Store purchases via Screen Time or using “Ask to Buy” in Family Sharing.

08Privacy at a glance

  • No user account with us, no sign-in with email.
  • Avatar, hearts and settings are stored on the device. In online mode, the avatar and game data are sent to the other players.
  • No access to camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Apple's and Google's privacy policies also apply to Game Center and advertising.

More details can be found in the Privacy Policy in the App's settings.

09How to set up the iPhone or iPad

Limit playing time: Settings → Screen Time → App Limits. Here you can set daily limits for the App or the “Games” category. There is also “Downtime”.

Restrict online multiplayer: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → App Store, Media, Web & Games → Game Center. There you can allow, limit to friends or block multiplayer games, adding friends, private messages and “Nearby Multiplayer”, among other things.

Prevent purchases: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases. Alternatively, you can set up “Ask to Buy” in Family Sharing.

Prevent tracking requests: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. There you can turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”. Onpoys MiniGames only asks verified users aged 16+ about tracking anyway.

Sharing the age range: In Family Sharing, you can decide whether your child's age range is shared with apps.

Many of these settings can also be managed from your own device via Family Sharing. The exact names may differ slightly depending on the iOS version.

10Reporting inappropriate players

As the App has no reporting or blocking function of its own, you or your child can report suspicious Game Center profiles directly to Apple: open your avatar in Apple's “Games” app, select recently met players under “All Friends” and submit a report via “More Options” → “Report Player”. You can also let us know at any time using the contact details below.

11Playing safely

  • Some mini-games are controlled by tilting the device. Your child should stay seated or stand still and pay attention to their surroundings.
  • The App uses vibration and bright, fast light effects. If your child is known to be light-sensitive or has epilepsy, please talk to a doctor first.
  • Regular breaks are good. Music, sounds and vibration can be turned off in the settings under “Audio”.
