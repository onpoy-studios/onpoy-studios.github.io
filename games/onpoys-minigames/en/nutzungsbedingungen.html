02Game and features

“Onpoys MiniGames” is a free party game with 25 short mini-games. You can:

  • play offline against two computer-controlled opponents,
  • play online via Apple Game Center against random players or invited friends,
  • design your own player avatar.

You do not need a user account for offline mode. Online mode requires an internet connection and the use of Apple Game Center.

The App currently contains no in-app purchases or other paid game content.

The App has no text or voice chat of its own.

We cannot guarantee a permanently available internet connection, successful matchmaking or uninterrupted online matches. In particular, disruptions at Apple Game Center, network problems or incompatible app versions may affect use.

Statutory claims in the event of performance failures remain unaffected.

05Hearts and game rules

Online mode uses a virtual heart system:

  • Each online match you start uses up one heart.
  • You can have a maximum of four hearts.
  • Used hearts refill automatically, currently one every 30 minutes.
  • Where optional rewarded videos are available, you can get an extra heart by watching one.
  • If you voluntarily leave an online match early, the heart used remains spent.

Hearts are purely a game feature. They have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, transferred or exchanged for money.

08Health and safety

Motion controls: Some mini-games are controlled by tilting or moving your device. Hold your device securely and pay attention to your surroundings. Do not play in traffic or in dangerous situations.

Light and flash effects: The App contains fast movements, bright colours and some flashing light effects. Such stimuli may cause discomfort in light-sensitive people or, in rare cases, trigger epileptic seizures. If you have such a condition or are unsure, seek medical advice before use.

Feeling unwell: Stop playing immediately if you feel dizzy, get a headache or notice any other discomfort.

Take regular breaks. You can turn off music, sound effects and vibration in the game settings.
