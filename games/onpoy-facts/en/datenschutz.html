Below we explain which data is processed when you use the App, for what purposes this happens, and what rights users have regarding their personal data.

02General Information on the App and Data Processing

OnPoy Facts is a bluff and quiz game for iOS. No registration and no user account of our own are required to use it. We do not operate our own backend or server; game progress and statistics are not transmitted to us.

The App itself in particular does not collect email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, photos, videos or voice recordings. The exception is player names that users voluntarily enter in offline mode (Section 4). For the online modes, the App uses Apple Game Center (Sections 5 and 6). Google AdMob is integrated for the advertising that finances the App. Personalized advertising and tracking only take place if users expressly consent (Sections 7 to 11).

03Data Stored Locally on the Device

The App stores the following information exclusively locally on the device (Apple UserDefaults):

  • Settings: music on/off, music volume, button sounds, vibration and language,
  • Offline mode statistics: player names and, for each name, the number of games, wins, points, truths found, successful bluffs and timeouts; also the total number of games and rounds,
  • a history of questions already asked, so that questions are not repeated too soon,
  • the number of lives and the time at which the next life is restored ("Random Opponent" mode),
  • the most recently used names of computer opponents, so that the same name does not always appear,
  • technical interim states for open online games (e.g. answers already selected before they are sent to Game Center).

This storage is strictly necessary for the game functions requested by the user (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG, German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act); the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the App and its game functions). The App itself does not permanently store any advertising-related information; how often video ads appear is only counted in memory while the App is in use. Information stored on the device by the Google Mobile Ads SDK or the consent management is described in Sections 7 to 10.

Local data is not transmitted to us. We cannot attribute it to any person and cannot access or delete it remotely. The statistics can be deleted in the App under "Statistik → Statistik zurücksetzen" (Statistics → Reset statistics); the remaining local data cannot be deleted individually and is removed by deleting the App.

04Player Names and Word Filter

In offline mode, users can enter freely chosen names or nicknames to distinguish players on the same device. These names are stored locally on the device for the statistics (Section 3) and are not transmitted to us or third parties. We recommend using nicknames instead of full names.

Entered names are checked exclusively on the device by a word filter for offensive terms, contact details and emojis. Nothing is transmitted or stored in the process. In the game, answers are selected exclusively from predefined texts; there is no free text input between players.

05Apple Game Center

The App is connected to Apple Game Center. When the App starts, the Game Center sign-in status is checked and, if necessary, Apple's Game Center sign-in is displayed. The actual multiplayer functions are only used once an online mode ("Zufälliger Gegner" / Random Opponent or "Mit Freunden" / With Friends) is selected. Without signing in, the online modes cannot be used; offline mode remains fully available.

In the online modes, the following data in particular is processed:

  • the Game Center name (alias) and a Game Center player ID,
  • in "Random Opponent" mode: the opponent search via Game Center and the game state (questions, selected answers, votes, points and players' aliases), which Apple stores so that play can continue in turns; also rematch requests,
  • in "With Friends" mode: invitations and the game data transmitted between devices during the live game (selected answers, votes, points).

Other players see the Game Center name and the game results. The App does not display profile pictures. Notifications that it is your turn again are provided by Apple via Game Center. We ourselves only receive this data within the App and do not store it on our own servers.

According to Apple, for signed-in Game Center users Apple also collects information such as game activity, other players and usage data, and generally retains Game Center data for as long as the associated Apple Account exists. Game Center is a service provided by Apple (for users in the EEA: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland); Apple's Privacy Policy and Apple's information on Game Center and privacy apply. Game Center settings can be managed in the iOS Settings under "Game Center".

The legal basis for using Game Center in the App is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the online games chosen by the user).

06Computer Opponents

Before a game starts in "Random Opponent" mode, the App searches for a human opponent via Apple Game Center (Section 5). If no opponent is found within a short time, any Game Center match that may have been created in the process is ended or removed, and a computer opponent ("QuizBot") plays instead. The subsequent game against the computer opponent runs locally on the device; no game data is transmitted to other players or to us.

07Google AdMob and Advertising

The App is free of charge and financed by advertising. For this we use Google AdMob via the Google Mobile Ads SDK. For users in the EEA, the provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

The following are used:

  • banner ads on the home screen,
  • full-screen video ads (interstitials) after every third completed game in offline mode and in "With Friends" mode,
  • voluntary reward videos (rewarded ads): anyone who has no lives left in "Random Opponent" mode can actively choose to watch a video to receive an additional life.

In connection with the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google may in particular process the following data: IP address, device and app identifiers, the device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only with tracking permission, Section 11), approximate location derived from the IP address, information about the device, operating system and app, information about ads displayed and interactions with them, as well as performance and diagnostic data.

The processing serves to deliver and select ads, limit ad frequency, measurement and billing, fraud and abuse prevention, security and – only with consent (Sections 8 and 11) – the personalization of advertising based on interests and data from other apps and websites. Google processes some of this data under its own responsibility. Further information: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and How Google retains data.

08Consent, Personalized and Limited Ads

Before the Google Mobile Ads SDK stores or reads information on the device, and before any personalization of advertising, consent is obtained via Google's consent form (Section 9) (Section 25(1) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). "Do not consent" is just as easy to choose as "Consent".

  • With consent (and, for the use of the IDFA, with tracking permission under Section 11), Google can personalize advertising.
  • Without consent, only non-personalized or limited ads are shown. Even then, technical data under Section 7 may be processed insofar as this is necessary and legally permissible for delivery, fraud prevention and security.

The App can be used without restriction in all cases.

09Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

We use the Google User Messaging Platform to manage consent. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are informed about data processing for advertising purposes when they first start the App and are asked for their decision. The decision is stored locally on the device and made available to the advertising services involved where necessary.

10Changing and Withdrawing Consent

Consent given can be changed or withdrawn at any time with effect for the future – in the App under "Einstellungen → Werbeeinstellungen" (Settings → Ad settings). Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of the processing carried out up to that point.

11Tracking Permission (App Tracking Transparency, IDFA)

On first launch, the App uses Apple's system prompt ("App Tracking Transparency") to ask whether it may track activity across other companies' apps and websites. Google's consent form (Section 9) appears afterwards.

Only if tracking permission is granted may the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) be used and data from this App be linked with data from other apps and websites for advertising purposes (tracking as defined by Apple). If permission is denied, no such tracking takes place; the App remains fully usable.

The permission can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings under "Privacy & Security → Tracking".

12No Camera, No Microphone, No GPS

The App does not require access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location and does not request these permissions. This is distinct from an approximate location that Google may derive from the IP address (Section 7).

13No User Accounts and No Chat Functions

The App has no user account of its own, no public profiles, no chat, no private messages and no way to share photos, videos or your own texts with other players. In the online modes, only the Game Center name and game data are shared with other players (Section 5).

14Disclosure of Personal Data

We do not sell personal data. Processing by third parties only takes place within the scope of the services described, in particular by Google (AdMob, UMP) and Apple (App Store, iOS, Game Center). Beyond this, we do not pass on personal data to third parties unless we are legally obliged to do so.

15Data Transfers to Third Countries

Google and Apple may process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the USA. For transfers to the USA, there is an adequacy decision of the EU Commission (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, Art. 45 GDPR), insofar as the recipient is certified under it; otherwise, the EU Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR) are used. Details can be found in the providers' privacy information.

16Legal Bases

Depending on the processing, it is based on:

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: provision of the App, the game functions and the selected online modes (Sections 3 to 6),
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR in conjunction with Section 25(1) TDDDG: consent to the storage and reading of information on the device, to personalized advertising and to tracking (Sections 8 and 11),
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: legitimate interest in delivering non-personalized or limited ads and in security and fraud prevention, insofar as no consent is required (Sections 7 and 8),
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR: compliance with legal obligations.

For storing information on the device, Section 25 TDDDG also applies: strictly necessary storage takes place under Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG (Section 3), all other storage only with consent (Section 25(1) TDDDG).

17Storage Period and Deletion

Locally stored data remains on the device until it is deleted using a function provided for this purpose (e.g. "Statistik zurücksetzen" / Reset statistics) or the App is uninstalled. Not all local data can be deleted individually within the App. Data in Apple Game Center (Section 5) and at Google (Section 7) is processed according to the respective retention and deletion rules of these providers; Apple generally retains Game Center data for as long as the Apple Account exists. Advertising consent can be changed at any time (Section 10).

18Minors

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The App has no user accounts, no chat and no function for exchanging your own content with unknown players.

In Germany, young people can generally only give consent under data protection law themselves from the age of 16 (Art. 8 GDPR). Users under 16 should therefore only consent to personalized advertising and tracking (Sections 8 and 11) with their parents' approval; without consent, only non-personalized or limited ads are shown. We recommend that parents set up the settings provided by Apple for tracking, advertising, Game Center and Screen Time appropriately for their child's age (e.g. turning off "Allow Apps to Request to Track").

19Rights of Data Subjects

Where the GDPR applies, data subjects have in particular the following rights:

  • access (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • objection (Art. 21 GDPR),
  • withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

We cannot attribute locally stored data (Section 3) to any person or delete it remotely, as we do not maintain user accounts or our own server. For data held by Apple Game Center (e.g. game states of turn-based online games) and by Google, these rights can also be exercised directly against Apple or Google.

21Security and Data Minimization

The App is designed according to the principle of data minimization: no user account of its own, no own server, no free texts between players. The technical and organizational security measures of Google and Apple also apply to their services.
