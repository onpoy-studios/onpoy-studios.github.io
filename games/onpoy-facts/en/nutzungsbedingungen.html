02Subject Matter of the App

OnPoy Facts is a bluff and quiz game. Every question has one surprising, true answer; players choose bluffs from predefined false answers to deceive their fellow players, and then vote on which answer is the truth. The App offers in particular:

  • Offline: 2 to 4 players on one device, 5, 10 or 15 questions,
  • Online "Random Opponent": 2 players, 5 questions, taking turns via Apple Game Center,
  • Online "With Friends": up to 4 players simultaneously via Apple Game Center.

Changes to game modes, questions and content are governed by Section 13.

06Online Play via Apple Game Center

Signing in to Apple Game Center is required for the online modes. The availability of the online modes depends on Apple.

The following applies to "Random Opponent" mode (turn-based play):

  • If no other player is found within a short time, a computer opponent plays. Computer opponents can always be recognized by the name "QuizBot".
  • Points, wins and other results from games against computer opponents do not count towards leaderboards, rankings or achievements (e.g. in Game Center).
  • The active player has up to 24 hours for their turn. Anyone who lets this deadline pass loses the game.
  • A maximum of 5 games can be open at the same time. Finished games remain visible until the player leaves them.
  • After a game, a rematch can be requested; it starts when both players agree.

The following applies to all online modes: anyone who leaves an ongoing game during a selection may, depending on the mode, lose the game or be considered eliminated.

07Lives

In "Random Opponent" mode, users have up to 4 lives. Each new game costs one life, regardless of whether it is played against a human or a computer opponent. One life is restored every 30 minutes, up to a maximum of 4. In addition, users without lives can voluntarily watch a rewarded ad to receive a life; there is no entitlement to the availability of such an ad. Continuing open games and rematches do not cost a life.

Lives are purely a game element: they cannot be bought, sold or transferred, have no monetary value and are only stored on the respective device (they are lost when the App is deleted). Manipulating lives, e.g. by changing the device clock, is not permitted. Changes to the lives system are governed by Section 13.
